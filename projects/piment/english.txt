The country that eats the most chili in the world… only discovered it 500 years ago.
That country is India.
And the chili? It comes from Mexico.
There, people have grown it for about six thousand years.
In 1493, Christopher Columbus tastes it in the Caribbean.
He thinks he has found the pepper of the Indies… and calls it "pimiento": pepper.
Wrong country, wrong name. But the name will stick.
Then Portuguese sailors carry it everywhere.
Along the coasts of Africa… all the way to Goa, India, in the early 16th century.
Before that, in India, the heat came from black pepper.
The chili settles in the south first… and takes two centuries to reach the north.
In Goa it was even called "Pernambuco pepper", after a region of Brazil.
Today India is the world's largest producer, consumer and exporter of chili.
So next time a dish sets your mouth on fire…
thank Mexico.
