The first ketchup had no tomato… it was fermented fish.
In southern China, this sauce was called kê-tsiap.
In the early 1700s, English merchants taste it in Asia… and bring the idea home.
Without that fish, they improvise: mushroom, walnut, oyster ketchup.
In 1812, a Philadelphia scientist writes the first recipe… with tomato.
In 1876, Heinz bottles it. The fish is gone… but the name stayed.
And you, would you have tasted the original ketchup?
